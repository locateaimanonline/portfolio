10. Collaboration and team contributions

This chapter connects the support knowledge-base work with training, shared writing practice, and the AI authoring workflow. The recognition chapter and full feedback preserve colleagues' and managers' own words separately from my account.

10.1 Making knowledge reusable for colleagues

In Support, I helped agents format knowledge-base articles and communicated best practices with managers and team leads. In Technical Writing, I contributed review guidance, training materials, tool instructions, and onboarding feedback. The continuity is the effort to make a useful method accessible beyond the person who first discovered it.

The AI Documentation Workflow Training Guide's process and DITA Figure Wrapper instructions are concrete examples of this transfer. They package a process or utility for other writers rather than leaving its operation in an individual conversation.
