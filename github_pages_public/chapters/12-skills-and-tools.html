12. Skills and tools

The Skills page lets visitors browse the products, systems, and methods I use, alongside the roles where I have applied them. Here I explain how those tools support my work across investigation, writing, and documentation systems.

12.1 Capabilities supported by examples

I research product behavior, plan help changes, write and review DITA/XML topics, prepare screenshots and video guidance, and check rendered WebHelp/HTML5 output. Oxygen, Git, Jira, Camtasia, and CSS support different parts of that process. The writing chapter shows how the work moves from research to guidance readers can follow.

In support roles, I combined system administration, enterprise mobility, customer troubleshooting, log and database investigation, identity integration, server maintenance, and escalation support. Product expertise connects those capabilities to the device platforms and products I worked with.

As a senior writer, I have extended the work into transformations, migration, rendered review, publishing configuration, validation inventories, AI-assisted development, and cross-system integration. The Projects page shows how these methods address specific authoring and quality problems.

12.3 AI and engineering in practice

AI tools have become central to how I build and improve documentation systems. I use ChatGPT to assist with drafting and review. With Codex, I developed repeatable workflows that connect Jira and design research to DITA authoring, source checks, and peer review. I used Claude for migration-related development and adapted reusable skills for both Codex and Claude. The AI workflow chapter explains how I assess generated content before a writer uses it.

I have built task-focused agents with Microsoft Copilot and Copilot Studio, and automation flows with Power Automate. These workflows can respond to email or new worksheet and tracker entries, update records, and send notifications. I have also investigated Open Web UI for AI integrations and local workflow design.

Python and PowerShell help me inspect repositories, build utilities, and automate repeatable checks. I have worked with DITA-OT, Ant, and XSLT questions when investigating transformations, and with Umbraco, Pulse, and IIS when checking how structured content becomes reliable online help.

Jira and Figma provide feature and design context. Git/GitHub Enterprise preserves change history and review decisions. I have also explored Excel/Jira Data Center data and Aha exports for team reporting. These tools serve different parts of the same process: understand the product change, create guidance, verify the result, and make the method reusable.
