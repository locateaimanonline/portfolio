Manager feedback

These comments are from a manager evaluation shared in June 2026.

Accountability

Aiman consistently exemplifies a high level of accountability in every aspect of his work. He not only takes full ownership of his assigned tasks but also proactively steps in to improve processes, support teammates, and elevate the overall quality of our documentation. His initiative in optimizing OxygenXML performance and updating the interactive network diagram shows a deep sense of responsibility for the tools and systems we rely on. Beyond his technical contributions, Aiman has taken on an unofficial leadership role, coordinating complex documentation projects, managing timelines, and guiding other writers with clarity and professionalism.

His ability to anticipate challenges, follow through on commitments, and take initiative beyond his formal responsibilities sets a strong example for the team. Aiman’s consistent follow-through and willingness to take calculated risks in pursuit of better outcomes make him an invaluable asset and a standout contributor in this category.

Innovation

Aiman demonstrates a forward-thinking approach that goes beyond simply completing tasks, he actively looks for ways to improve how the team works and delivers value. His initiatives reflect a clear understanding of both the technical landscape and the team's broader goals. By introducing tools and enhancements that streamline workflows and improve content accuracy, Aiman not only boosts efficiency but also reduces friction in collaborative processes. His work shows a strong alignment between innovation and practical impact.

What stands out most is Aiman’s ability to connect innovation with measurable outcomes. The BI report he developed, for example, reflects a strategic mindset, using data to inform decisions and highlight the value of support resources. Rather than innovating for its own sake, Aiman focuses on solutions that are sustainable and scalable, demonstrating maturity in how he evaluates and executes new ideas. His contributions help build a culture of continuous improvement and thoughtful experimentation within the team.

Customer Focus

Aiman demonstrates a strong commitment to enhancing the customer experience by proactively identifying opportunities to add value. His collaboration with the Support team to analyze case resolution data highlights his focus on understanding how documentation impacts customer outcomes. By quantifying the effectiveness of Online Help references, he provides actionable insights that directly support customer success.

In addition, Aiman shows initiative in improving the support journey by recommending the integration of instructional video content into response templates. This not only empowers users to resolve issues independently but also strengthens the connection between documentation and customer support. His ongoing engagement with technical support contacts to gather feedback further reflects his dedication to continuous improvement and his ability to balance customer needs with business goals.

Quality

Aiman consistently demonstrates a strong commitment to quality, with a clear focus on accuracy, consistency, and continuous improvement. His development of the Oxygen XML Troubleshooting guide reflects a proactive approach to capturing and resolving recurring issues, helping to reduce friction for both writers and users. By creating a DITA task template and example, he has also contributed to raising the structural and stylistic consistency of documentation across the team.

Aiman’s work reflects a thoughtful, methodical approach to quality that goes beyond meeting expectations—he actively raises the bar for excellence.

SOTI Spirit

Aiman exemplifies the SOTI Spirit through his active involvement, cross-functional collaboration, and genuine commitment to the broader company culture. He consistently goes beyond the scope of his role, contributing to initiatives that enhance both operational efficiency and employee experience. His work with the Knowledge Base Management Committee, including the development of best practices and process transparency, reflects a dedication to continuous improvement and knowledge sharing across teams.

What truly sets Aiman apart is his ability to build strong, professional relationships across departments. He is a natural connector who fosters collaboration and goodwill, making him a trusted and respected presence throughout the organization. His contributions to the First Aid Committee and emergency preparedness efforts further demonstrate his investment in the well-being of his colleagues. Aiman is a role model of the SOTI Spirit—engaged, collaborative, and always striving to elevate those around him.

Manager Summary

This year, Aiman has made significant contributions across multiple areas, consistently demonstrating quiet leadership and a strong collaborative spirit. He steps in where needed, shares his knowledge generously, and supports the success of others without seeking recognition. His work has had a meaningful impact on documentation quality, cross-departmental collaboration, and internal process improvements. What stands out most is his thoughtful, steady approach to problem-solving and his ability to build strong, professional relationships across teams. As he continues to grow in his role, I encourage Aiman to seek out more visible opportunities to lead or influence cross-functional initiatives. His approach is a valuable asset to the team and to SOTI as a whole. With this strong foundation, I would like to begin preparing Aiman for a transition to the Technical Writer II level next year through a focused performance development plan.
